# Working Capital and Cash Flow

Use this skill for liquidity analysis, working capital diagnostics, cash forecasting, or CFO cash improvement plans.

## Workflow

1. Profile cash and working capital:
   - Receivables, payables, inventory, deferred revenue, accrued expenses, capex, debt, tax.

2. Calculate metrics:
   - DSO, DPO, DIO, cash conversion cycle, operating cash flow, free cash flow, liquidity runway.

3. Identify drivers:
   - Payment terms, collections, overdue debtors, inventory turns, supplier timing, seasonality, one-off receipts/payments.

4. Build forecast:
   - Opening cash, receipts, payments, payroll, suppliers, tax, capex, debt service, closing cash.

5. Recommend actions:
   - Collections, payment timing, inventory reduction, supplier negotiation, capex deferral, financing actions.

## Output Format

Return:
- Cash/working capital dashboard
- Metric calculations
- Driver diagnosis
- Forecast assumptions
- Action plan

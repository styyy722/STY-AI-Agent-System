# Pricing and Margin Analysis

Use this skill for price change decisions, margin improvement, discount analysis, profitability diagnostics, and customer/product economics.

## Workflow

1. Define scope:
   - Product, customer, channel, region, contract, SKU, period, and currency.

2. Calculate profitability:
   - Revenue, discount, net revenue, COGS, gross margin, variable cost, contribution margin, cost-to-serve.

3. Diagnose margin drivers:
   - Price, volume, mix, discounting, input costs, freight, labour, FX, rebates, returns, service intensity.

4. Evaluate pricing decision:
   - Price elasticity assumptions.
   - Break-even volume loss.
   - Customer/segment impact.
   - Competitive and contract constraints.

5. Recommend:
   - Price increase, discount guardrails, SKU rationalisation, customer renegotiation, cost action.

## Output Format

Return:
- Margin bridge
- Profitability table
- Pricing sensitivity
- Recommendation
- Risks and controls

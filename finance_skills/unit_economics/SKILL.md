# Unit Economics

Use this skill for SaaS metrics, recurring revenue models, customer profitability, product economics, and investor/CFO analysis.

## Workflow

1. Define unit:
   - Customer, account, subscription, transaction, product, user, or cohort.

2. Calculate revenue metrics:
   - MRR, ARR, ARPU, ARPA, expansion, contraction, churn, NRR, GRR.

3. Calculate cost metrics:
   - CAC, sales & marketing cost, onboarding cost, support cost, COGS, gross margin, contribution margin.

4. Calculate economics:
   - LTV, CAC payback, LTV/CAC, contribution margin per unit, cohort profitability.

5. Interpret:
   - Identify whether growth is profitable, payback is acceptable, churn is manageable, and segments are attractive.

## Output Format

Return:
- Metric definitions
- Calculation table
- Segment/cohort comparison
- Risks and caveats
- Recommended actions

# Three-Statement Model

Use this skill for corporate finance modelling, valuation, lender models, business cases, and multi-year forecasting.

## Workflow

1. Structure statements:
   - Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement, supporting schedules, assumptions, scenarios.

2. Build supporting schedules:
   - Revenue, COGS, opex, headcount, capex, depreciation, working capital, debt, interest, tax.

3. Link statements:
   - Net income flows to retained earnings.
   - Depreciation, working capital, capex, debt, and equity movements flow to cash flow.
   - Cash closes the balance sheet.

4. Validate:
   - Balance sheet balances.
   - Cash bridge reconciles.
   - Debt and interest circularity is handled deliberately.
   - Ratios and margins are plausible.

## Output Format

Return:
- Model architecture
- Required schedules
- Core formulas
- Integrity checks
- Scenario/sensitivity design

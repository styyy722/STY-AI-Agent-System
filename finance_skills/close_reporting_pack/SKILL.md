# Close Reporting Pack

Use this skill for month-end, quarter-end, management reporting, CFO packs, and board finance summaries.

## Workflow

1. Confirm reporting scope:
   - Period, entity, business unit, audience, materiality threshold, deadline, and required statements.

2. Prepare close checks:
   - Trial balance loaded, revenue cut-off, accruals, prepayments, payroll, intercompany, FX, eliminations, tax.

3. Build pack:
   - P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, KPI dashboard, variance bridge, forecast outlook, risks/opportunities.

4. Write commentary:
   - Lead with material movements.
   - Explain driver, recurrence, owner, action, and forecast impact.

5. Quality control:
   - Tie to source system.
   - Check sign convention.
   - Validate formulas and subtotals.

## Output Format

Return:
- Close pack structure
- Key checks
- Commentary draft
- Exceptions and owner actions
- CFO summary
